About this demo

A proof of concept of Burak for preventive health screening, set up for age-banded preventive screening packages (18–39, 40–64, 65–75), which each programme can configure.

  • Illustrative data only. Every person, value and date is fictional. Nothing here describes a real person.
  • Reference ranges. Based on published clinical guidelines and shown for illustration. In a live workspace each programme sets its own. Burak’s calculated scores and rule thresholds are illustrative until clinically signed off.
  • Decision support. Burak organises and flags results for review. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment.
  • Demo only. Built by Burakomics to show the workflow. Not for clinical use.
